# Design Principles

The app is built around a few rules:

1. **The file is the source of truth.** No hidden database, no migration screen.
2. **The first frame is still.** The graph settles only while you are looking at it.
3. **Text first.** Controls get out of the way of the page.
4. **Motion explains.** A panel opens where it came from; nothing bounces for decoration.

[[Typography]] handles the reading experience. [[Welcome]] is the tour.

## Colour

One accent carries selection, links and the active mode. Everything else is a step on the
neutral ramp. If two things are the same kind of thing, they look the same.
