# Local Graph

The local graph keeps the selected note and its immediate neighbours. It answers a different
question from the global map: not *what is in the vault*, but *what is around this idea*.

Open it from the links rail, or press `⌘L` while the graph is open to switch between **Global**
and **Local**.

## A reading path

Start at [[Knowledge Graph]], switch to Local, then click [[Focus]]. The map re-centres on a
smaller neighbourhood without losing the global layout.

Local views are also a good way to find orphan notes: a node with no neighbours is a note that
has not joined the conversation yet.
